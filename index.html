Why Made-to-Measure Makes a Difference

Off-the-shelf blinds are cut to a few common sizes, so they are trimmed, shimmed or simply accepted with imperfect gaps. A made-to-order product is produced for the dimensions we record on site, which means cleaner edges, better privacy and a tidier look from the street and from inside.

Custom work also opens up more choices: slat width, wood tone, painted finishes, cord-free operation, motorization and mounting inside or outside the window frame.

  • Exact-fit sizing for every opening
  • Natural wood and faux wood options
  • Aluminum and vertical styles
  • Cordless lifting for safer homes
  • Optional motorized control
  • Colors chosen against your own walls
